# Income OS

A free, offline-first **AI Income Operating System**.

It is not another chatbot wrapper. It is a complete command center that:

- reverse-engineers a monthly income target into weekly work
- picks a viable offer from your skills and hours
- stores leads, payments, and content in the browser
- generates copy-ready operator prompts so **Grok** or **Gemini** can run the business for $0

No server. No API key. No subscription. Open `index.html` and work.

**Live file:** open [`index.html`](./index.html) after cloning, or enable GitHub Pages on this repo (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch `main` / root).

## What “run by an assistant” means

You are the owner. The model is the operator.

1. Fill in your profile and income goal inside the app.
2. Copy the **Master System Prompt** from the Operator tab.
3. Paste it into Grok (a saved conversation or custom instructions) or Gemini (a Gem).
4. Each day, copy that day’s brief from the app and paste it to the assistant.
5. The assistant returns outreach, content, proposals, product drafts, and delivery work.
6. You send, publish, invoice, and collect. Log results in the Pipeline and Ledger.

The assistant never needs paid APIs. Free Grok and free Gemini are enough.

## Quick start

1. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
2. Complete **Setup**.
3. Review the generated **Plan**.
4. Open **Operator** → copy the Master System Prompt into Grok or Gemini.
5. Work the **Daily** tab every morning.
6. Log money in **Ledger** and deals in **Pipeline**.

## Zero-cost stack this app assumes

- **Brain:** Grok and/or Gemini (free tiers)
- **Storefront:** Gumroad or PayPal.Me
- **Docs / delivery:** Google Docs, Notion free, or plain Markdown
- **Design:** Canva free
- **Hosting:** this HTML file, or GitHub Pages
- **Outreach:** email you already have, LinkedIn, Facebook groups, local Facebook Marketplace, Nextdoor, Craigslist services

You can add paid tools later. Do not add them until the free stack has produced a payment.

## Realistic paths the engine recommends

The planner prefers **productized services** first (fastest cash), then **digital products** (leverage), then **content + affiliate** (slowest, highest ceiling). Hybrid is the default for most people with 8–20 hours a week.

Typical first offers the engine will push you toward:

- AI content packs for local businesses ($297–$797)
- SOP / process documentation ($197–$497)
- Listing, resume, or proposal writing ($47–$197)
- Local “AI receptionist / review reply / Google Business” retainer ($199–$499/mo)
- A $19–$49 template pack sold on Gumroad

## Honesty

This app will not print money by itself. Assistants draft. You ship. Income shows up when outreach and delivery happen on a calendar, not when prompts look impressive.

Export your data from Settings before you clear the browser.
