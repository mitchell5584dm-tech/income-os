# Operator jobs

Paste the in-app Master System Prompt first, then one job.

## Offer architect
Rewrite the flagship offer so a busy owner understands it in 20 seconds. Include who, painful before, result after, 3 deliverables, price, 14-day guarantee, first-step CTA.

## Lead list builder
20 specific lead targets in or near the Owner’s city. Business type, this-week pain, where to find them, one-line angle.

## Outreach writer
8 variants: 3 email, 3 Facebook/Nextdoor, 2 LinkedIn. 80–130 words. One ask.

## Proposal factory
One-page proposal from the conversation: problem, 5-bullet scope, timeline, price, exclusions, next step.

## Delivery engine
First-delivery pack: checklist, client questions, finished example, 60-minute work script.

## Product mill
One $19–$49 Gumroad product that is a byproduct of the service. Full outline + sales blurb.

## Content that closes
5 posts for the best channel. Belief + proof + soft CTA to the flagship.

## Price and package
Comparables, good/better/best, retainer version, what to cut when they hesitate.

## Objection killer
10 real objections, short replies, 3-email follow-up over 10 days.

## Weekly review
Constraint is traffic, conversion, delivery, or collection. One move for 7 days.
